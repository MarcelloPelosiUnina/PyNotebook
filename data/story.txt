The old lighthouse stood at the edge of the island.
Every night the keeper climbed the stairs and lit the lamp.
Ships passed in the dark, and the light showed them the way.
One winter night the lamp went out, and the keeper was afraid.
He climbed the stairs again, with a small candle in his hand.
The candle was weak, but the ships saw it, and the ships were safe.
In the morning the keeper smiled: "A small light is still a light."
